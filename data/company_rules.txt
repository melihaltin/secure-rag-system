### 1. Introduction and Welcome

Welcome to the TechFlow Inc. family. This handbook has been prepared to clarify our company’s cultural values, operational standards, and the rights and responsibilities of our employees. Our goal is to create a productive working environment centered on innovation and technology, based on mutual respect.

---

### 2. Working Hours and Work Arrangement

Maintaining a healthy work–life balance is a core principle at our company. Our standard working hours are as follows:

* **Weekdays:** Monday – Friday
* **Working Hours:** 09:00 – 18:00
* **Lunch Break:** A flexible 1-hour break that can be taken between 12:00 and 13:30.

TechFlow Inc. supports flexible working hours (Flex-Time) with managerial approval during project-based peak periods or when global meeting requirements arise. However, all teams are expected to be reachable during the designated **core hours (10:00–16:00)**.

---

### 3. Hybrid Working Policy (Remote Work)

In line with our technology-driven structure, and to balance office synergy with individual focus, a **Hybrid Working Model** is implemented.

* **Implementation:** 3 days in the office, 2 days working remotely per week.
* **Planning:** Remote working days must be coordinated with department managers and recorded in the weekly team calendar.
* **Accessibility:** On remote working days, employees must remain continuously reachable via Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Email during office hours (09:00–18:00), and must attend scheduled meetings with their cameras turned on.

---

### 4. Dress Code Policy

TechFlow Inc. values both a professional image and employee comfort. The general dress code applicable in the office is defined as **“Smart Casual.”**

**Acceptable Attire:**

* Fabric trousers, chino (linen) pants, dark-colored and non-ripped jeans.
* Shirts, polo shirts, blouses, knitwear, and jackets.
* Closed-toe shoes, loafers, and clean, simple sneakers.

**Unacceptable Attire:**

* Shorts, capri pants, tracksuits, leggings (unless worn with a long tunic).
* T-shirts containing slogans or political messages.
* Beach sandals and flip-flops.

**Note:** On days requiring customer meetings or official representation, **“Business Formal”** attire (e.g., suits) should be preferred.

---

### 5. Benefits: Meal Card Program

To support employees’ daily nutrition needs, the company provides a meal card/voucher.

* **System:** Meal payments are loaded onto the “TechCard” system (e.g., Setcard / Multinet / Sodexo).
* **Balance Loading:** Between the 1st and 5th of each month, balances are automatically loaded based on the actual number of working days in that month (calculated using the predetermined daily net amount).
* **Usage Areas:** The cards are valid at contracted restaurants as well as for grocery shopping.
* **Leave / Medical Report Status:** During annual leave or medical leave exceeding 3 days, the meal allowance for the relevant days will be deducted from the following month’s balance.
